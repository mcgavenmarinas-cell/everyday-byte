ACCOUNT SAFETY

How to make your online accounts safer

Use a unique password for every important account, turn on two-step verification, and keep recovery details up to date. A password manager can help you create and store strong passwords without reusing the same one everywhere.

SMARTPHONE TIPS

Easy ways to free up phone storage

Start by checking your phone's storage settings to see what's taking up space. Remove apps you no longer use, clear downloaded files you recognize and no longer need, and back up photos before deleting local copies.

3 minute read · Starter guide

WEB BASICS

How to spot a suspicious website

Check the spelling of the domain, be careful with urgent messages, and avoid entering passwords after following unexpected links. A padlock means the connection is encrypted; it does not prove that a website is trustworthy.
